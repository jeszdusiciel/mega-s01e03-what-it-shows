0002 · Triage by failing path, not subject

owner Kajetan Szyba adopted for this review reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

Replaces decision 0001 for this review. The old accepted record is preserved. The incident review shows why matching subjects merged separate failures into one ticket.

Decision

Read the body and identify tenant, tenant locale, form version and channel. Merge only when evidence supports the same failing path. Compare the supplied cause logic with the symptoms; do not silently override contradictory evidence. Keep unresolved cases for investigation. Browser locale, intake tag and subject alone cannot establish cause.

Count unique report IDs. Preserve received and merged timestamps independently. Keep one engineering issue per confirmed problem and link related issues rather than merging them just because they produce a similar symptom.

The same tenant on app versus email can share a cause despite different subjects. The same subject and tenant on form versus app can have different causes. Identical subjects on different tenants’ forms can differ by form version. Unknown tenants do not have an established cause and must not be merged on that basis.

Consequences

Triage takes more reading and tenant lookup. It keeps a smaller issue from disappearing inside a larger ticket. New reports continue to arrive: recheck the data and ticket assignments instead of treating a “known” label as evidence that the failures stopped.
